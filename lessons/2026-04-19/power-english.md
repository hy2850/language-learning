# Making a Career Change

## 1. Warm-up

Callie has landed three clients for her new business and is ready to go independent. Starting your own business is both exciting and terrifying — the pressure is real, but so is the thrill of building something that's entirely yours.

칼리가 새 사업을 위한 고객 세 명을 확보하고 독립할 준비를 합니다. 창업은 설레면서도 무섭지만, 자기 힘으로 무언가를 만들어간다는 짜릿함도 함께 옵니다.

---

## 2. Expressions

**1. strike out on your own**
- To leave a group or organization and start doing something independently
- 독립해서 나가다, 홀로서기를 하다

**2. build something from the ground up**
- To create something from nothing, starting from the very beginning with no existing foundation
- 맨땅에서부터 만들어 올리다, 처음부터 쌓아 올리다

**3. raise the stakes**
- To increase the risk involved in a situation, with the potential for both greater reward and greater loss
- 판돈을 올리다, 리스크를 키우다

## 3. Power Dialogue

**Callie:** I've already got three clients for my new business, so I'm officially going to strike out on my own. It's exciting and terrifying.
**Blake:** That's huge! You're really starting to build something from the ground up.
**Callie:** Every new project feels like it raises the stakes, but I feel alive again.
**Blake:** Pressure isn't always bad when it pushes you forward. How do you feel about it?
**Callie:** I finally feel challenged in a good way.
**Blake:** That's usually a sign you're doing the right thing.

## 5. Notes

### strike out on your own
- To leave a company, team, or group you were part of and begin doing something independently. It implies leaving a stable situation to pursue your own path.
- **A:** Why did you leave the company?
  **B:** I wanted to strike out on my own.
- She struck out on her own as a consultant after ten years at the firm.

### build something from the ground up
- To create something entirely from scratch, with no pre-existing foundation or resources to rely on. Often used for businesses, careers, or organizations.
- **A:** Did you buy an existing company?
  **B:** No, I built it from the ground up.
- He built his career from the ground up, starting with odd jobs and slowly building a reputation.

### raise the stakes
- To increase the level of risk in a situation, which also increases the potential reward or consequence. Originally a gambling term now used in everyday language.
- **A:** Why expand so fast?
  **B:** It raises the stakes, but the rewards are bigger.
- Opening a second location raised the stakes, but it doubled their revenue within a year.

---

## 6. Power Pattern

### That's huge!
- Used to react to someone's accomplishment or big news with excitement and admiration. It expresses that something is a significant, impressive achievement — almost always positive.
- 대단하다! 엄청난 일이다!

**Examples:**
1. Most businesses never become profitable that fast. That's huge!
2. A contract with such a huge company will bring in more sales. That's huge!

---

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### terrifying
- Extremely frightening; causing intense fear or dread. Much stronger than "scary" — it implies you are shaking with fear or deeply unsettled.
- 무시무시한, 겁이 날 정도로 무서운

**Examples:**
1. Starting a business with no safety net is terrifying, but she did it anyway.
2. The thought of speaking in front of a thousand people is absolutely terrifying.
