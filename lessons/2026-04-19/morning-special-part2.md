## 2. Today's Idiom
- Idiom: carry the can
- Korean meaning: 모든 책임을 지다

### Setup
PD의 생일 파티를 준비하는 상황. 파티 장소가 Riverside Hall에서 Riverstone Hall로 변경됐는데, Paul이 다른 사람들에게 장소 변경을 전달하지 않아서 혼선이 발생함. Sue 역을 최수진이, Paul 역을 Paul이 맡음.

### Dialogue
**Sue:** Does this dip look okay to you? I double checked the labels to make sure there's no dairy and I went light on the salt. The PD is so sensitive to both.
**Paul:** Oh, it's perfect. Better safe than sorry with those allergies. Is the chicken seasoned the same way?
**Sue:** Exactly the same. And the thing is...
**Paul:** Hold on! Oh, I just got a text from Shane. He says he's almost here with the pizza.
**Sue:** Oh good man! It must have been a nightmare finding a place that does a decent pizza without any cheese.
**Paul:** I hope it actually tastes like something. Shane swore the vegan place downtown is great. Did you finish the playlist? We need the vibe to be just right when the producer walks in.
**Sue:** No, don't worry — it's all set. I've got the upbeat tracks for the arrival and the surprise video greeting queued up right after, and the footage from the guests who couldn't make it is actually really touching.
**Paul:** Glad we got those clips. Oh, and keep an eye on the door; the flowers should be delivered any minute now.
**Sue:** Wait, Paul? What time is it?
**Paul:** It's almost seven. Why?
**Sue:** Why is nobody here yet? Shane said he was almost here. Have you heard anything from the others about their ETA?
**Paul:** Nothing. I figured they were all just carpooling together and running a bit late.
**Sue:** Wait! Shane just texted again. He says "I'm here. Where are you guys?"
**Paul:** What does he mean? He's here.
**Sue:** Where did you see him come in?
**Paul:** No. Is he at the front door? Hold on. Let me call him. Shane. Uh yeah we're inside.
**Shane:** What do you mean everyone's arrived? We're here. We're waving right now.
**Paul:** What? You can't see me. Everyone here at the Riverstone Hall can see me.
**Shane:** The what? You went to the Riverside Hall, not the Riverstone Hall. Didn't Paul tell you the venue changed to Riverstone, not Riverside?
**Sue:** What? Nobody heard that. Okay, stay there. I'll call you back. Paul, Paul.
**Paul:** Yeah?
**Sue:** Shane says everyone is at Riverside Hall. Everyone! How is that possible? I told you to tell them the venue is Riverstone Hall.
**Paul:** Ahh, you never told me to tell everyone the venue was here at Riverstone Hall.
**Sue:** Yes, I did. I specifically told you to tell everyone that Riverside Hall doesn't have a big screen for the video greeting, so we moved the party to Riverstone.
**Paul:** No, you told me Riverside Hall doesn't have a big screen, but it's okay because Riverstone Hall does. That's it! You never told me to tell everyone that we actually changed places.
**Sue:** Okay, look then — we need to find a way to pack all this food and the decorations and get over to Riverside Hall where the people actually are.
**Paul:** Are you kidding? Don't you think it's easier to just have everyone come here?
**Sue:** Oh yeah, actually that's a much better idea. Can you call Shane? Tell him to bring everyone here and apologize profusely. I'll carry the can for the mix-up. Meanwhile, I need to find a way to get the producer over here because I'm pretty sure she has no idea we're even having a party.

### Meaning
어떤 실수나 사고에 대해 책임을 지고 비난을 감수한다는 뜻. 군대나 해군에서 유래된 것으로 추정되며, 무거운 것을 들고 다니는 사람이 벌을 받는 사람이라는 의미에서 비롯됨. 영국, 호주, 캐나다에서 주로 사용하며, 미국에서는 "take the fall"을 더 많이 씀.

### Example Usages
- He made the error but she was left to carry the can.
- She carried the can to protect her husband from blame.
- Taxpayers will carry the can for the renovations.
- I'm not going to carry the can for your poor decisions.
- The junior manager had to carry the can for the team's collective mistakes.

### Similar Expressions
- take the fall
- take the rap
- take the heat
- take the hit
- be the scapegoat

## 4. Vocabulary and Useful Sentences

### ill-advised
- Meaning: poorly thought out; resulting from bad advice or judgment
- Korean: 현명하지 못한, 잘못된 조언에 따른
- Useful sentence: It would be ill-advised to invest all your savings in a single stock.
- Sentence Korean: 전 재산을 하나의 주식에 투자하는 것은 현명하지 못한 일이다.
- Note: 반대말은 well-advised. 팟캐스트 제목 "Ill Advised by Bill Nighy"는 의도적으로 자조적인 뉘앙스를 담고 있음.

### self-deprecating
- Meaning: making fun of yourself; claiming to be less skilled or important than you really are
- Korean: 자기 비하적인, 자신을 낮추는
- Useful sentence: The British are famously self-deprecating; take their modesty with a pinch of salt.
- Sentence Korean: 영국 사람들은 자기 비하적 표현을 잘 쓰는 것으로 유명하니, 그들의 겸손을 너무 그대로 받아들이지 마세요.
- Note: 영국 문화에서 매우 중요한 유머 스타일. Hugh Grant, Bill Nighy 등이 대표적. 한국의 자랑(chatang) 문화와 대비됨.

### squander
- Meaning: to waste something (time, money, opportunity) that could have been put to good use
- Korean: 낭비하다, 허비하다
- Useful sentence: Don't squander your money on textbooks and dictionaries — save it until you can buy dozens of doughnuts.
- Sentence Korean: 교과서와 사전에 돈을 낭비하지 마 — 나중에 도넛을 잔뜩 살 수 있도록 아껴둬.
- Note: 다소 격식 있는 단어로 일상 대화보다는 글이나 격식체에서 더 자주 쓰임. wasting보다 강한 뉘앙스.

### mellifluous
- Meaning: smooth, sweet, and pleasant to listen to (especially a voice or sound)
- Korean: (목소리가) 부드럽고 감미로운
- Useful sentence: Nighy attributes the podcast's success to his mellifluous voice.
- Sentence Korean: Nighy는 팟캐스트의 성공 비결이 자신의 감미로운 목소리 덕분일 거라고 했다.
- Note: 철자 M-E-L-L-I-F-L-U-O-U-S. 주로 목소리나 음악을 묘사할 때 사용하는 고급 어휘.

### refer to ~ as ~
- Meaning: to call or describe something/someone as something
- Korean: ~을 ~라고 부르다/말하다
- Useful sentence: I refer to Sue as "Queen of EBS Radio" even though she hates it.
- Sentence Korean: 나는 Sue를 "EBS 라디오의 여왕"이라고 부르는데 본인은 싫어한다.
- Note: 격식/비격식 모두 사용 가능한 매우 유용한 표현.

### at ease
- Meaning: comfortable, relaxed; not anxious or tense
- Korean: 편안한, 마음이 놓이는
- Useful sentence: Sue almost always seems at ease when she's on the radio — confident, calm, and comfortable.
- Sentence Korean: Sue는 라디오를 할 때 거의 항상 편안해 보인다 — 자신감 있고, 차분하고, 편안하게.
- Note: "put someone at ease" = 누군가를 편안하게 해주다. 면접, 사교 상황에서 자주 쓰임.

### spontaneity
- Meaning: the quality of being able to do things immediately without planning; improvisation
- Korean: 즉흥성, 자발성
- Useful sentence: Bill Nighy's spontaneity is what makes the podcast so entertaining — the producers don't show him the questions in advance.
- Sentence Korean: Bill Nighy의 즉흥성이 팟캐스트를 그토록 재미있게 만드는 요소다 — 제작진이 질문을 미리 보여주지 않는다.
- Note: 형용사형은 spontaneous. "at the drop of a hat"(즉시)과 함께 쓰이기도 함.

### stand one's ground
- Meaning: to hold your position; to refuse to change your mind or back down
- Korean: 자기 입장을 고수하다, 한 발짝도 물러서지 않다
- Useful sentence: I'm standing my ground when it comes to generative AI. Others can do with it as they wish, but I refuse to use it.
- Sentence Korean: 생성형 AI에 관해서 나는 내 입장을 고수한다. 다른 사람들은 원하는 대로 하겠지만, 나는 사용을 거부한다.
- Note: 논쟁, 토론, 협상에서 양보하지 않을 때 자주 사용하는 표현.

### sitting around
- Meaning: relaxing and doing nothing important; being idle
- Korean: 빈둥거리다, 아무것도 안 하고 앉아 있다
- Useful sentence: Don't just sit around and do nothing — get back to work!
- Sentence Korean: 그냥 빈둥거리지 말고 — 얼른 일 해!
- Note: 단순히 "앉아 있다(sitting)"와 다르게, 아무 생산적인 활동 없이 시간을 보내는 뉘앙스.

### tick / check all the right boxes
- Meaning: to fulfill all the criteria, needs, or expectations for something
- Korean: 모든 조건을 만족시키다, 모든 기준에 부합하다
- Useful sentence: Sue, your resume ticked all the right boxes and your interview went swimmingly. We think you're perfect for the job.
- Sentence Korean: Sue, 이력서가 모든 조건을 충족했고 면접도 아주 잘 진행됐습니다. 이 자리에 딱 맞는 분이라고 생각합니다.
- Note: 긍정적("ticks all the boxes")으로도, 부정적("ticks no boxes" = 어떤 기준에도 맞지 않음)으로도 사용 가능. 영국식은 tick, 미국식은 check.

### in that regard
- Meaning: in that way; from that perspective
- Korean: 그런 점에 있어서, 그런 면에서
- Useful sentence: It ticks no boxes; it's radical in that regard.
- Sentence Korean: 어떤 기준에도 맞지 않는다; 그런 면에서 정말 파격적이다.
- Note: regard = the way we see something. 격식체에서 자주 쓰이는 전환 표현.

### respectable
- Meaning: dressed smartly and looking socially acceptable; worthy of respect (in appearance)
- Korean: 단정한, 깔끔한 (외모/차림새)
- Useful sentence: I'm not very good at looking respectable. The only suit I own doesn't fit me, and I've forgotten how to tie a tie.
- Sentence Korean: 나는 단정하게 차려입는 걸 잘 못한다. 정장이 하나밖에 없는데 안 맞고, 넥타이 묶는 법도 잊어버렸다.
- Note: respectful(행동이 예의 바른)과 구분할 것. respectable은 주로 외모/차림새에 대해, respectful은 태도/행동에 대해 사용.
