## 2. Today's Idiom
- Idiom: **carry the can**
- Korean meaning: 모든 책임을 지다

### Setup
PD의 생일 파티를 준비하는 상황. 파티 장소가 Riverside Hall에서 Riverstone Hall로 변경됐는데, Paul이 다른 사람들에게 장소 변경을 전달하지 않아서 혼선이 발생함.

### Dialogue
**Sue:** Does this dip look okay to you? I double checked the labels to make sure there's no dairy and I went light on the salt. The PD is so sensitive to both.
**Paul:** Oh, it's perfect. Better safe than sorry with those allergies. Is the chicken seasoned the same way?
**Sue:** Exactly the same. And the thing is...
**Paul:** Hold on! Oh, I just got a text from Shane. He says he's almost here with the pizza.
**Sue:** Oh good man! It must have been a nightmare finding a place that does a decent pizza without any cheese.
**Paul:** I hope it actually tastes like something. Shane swore the vegan place downtown is great. Did you finish the playlist? We need the vibe to be just right when the producer walks in.
**Sue:** No, don't worry — it's all set. I've got the upbeat tracks for the arrival and the surprise video greeting queued up right after.
**Paul:** Glad we got those clips. Oh, and keep an eye on the door; the flowers should be delivered any minute now.
**Sue:** Wait, Paul? What time is it?
**Paul:** It's almost seven. Why?
**Sue:** Why is nobody here yet? Shane said he was almost here.
**Paul:** Nothing. I figured they were all just carpooling together and running a bit late.
**Sue:** Wait! Shane just texted again. He says "I'm here. Where are you guys?"
**Paul:** What does he mean? He's here.
**Sue:** Shane says everyone is at Riverside Hall. Everyone! I told you to tell them the venue is Riverstone Hall.
**Paul:** You never told me to tell everyone the venue was here at Riverstone Hall.
**Sue:** Yes, I did. I specifically told you to tell everyone that Riverside Hall doesn't have a big screen, so we moved to Riverstone.
**Paul:** No, you told me Riverside Hall doesn't have a big screen, but Riverstone does. You never told me to tell everyone we changed places.
**Sue:** Okay — can you call Shane? Tell him to bring everyone here and apologize profusely. I'll carry the can for the mix-up.

### Meaning
어떤 실수나 사고에 대해 책임을 지고 비난을 감수한다는 뜻. 군대/해군에서 유래. 영국, 호주, 캐나다에서 주로 사용하며, 미국에서는 "take the fall"을 더 많이 씀.

### Example Usages
- He made the error but she was left to carry the can.
- She carried the can to protect her husband from blame.
- Taxpayers will carry the can for the renovations.
- I'm not going to carry the can for your poor decisions.
- The junior manager had to carry the can for the team's collective mistakes.

### Similar Expressions
- take the fall
- take the rap
- take the heat
- be the scapegoat

## 4. Vocabulary and Useful Sentences

### ill-advised
- Meaning: poorly thought out; resulting from bad advice or judgment
- Korean: 현명하지 못한, 잘못된 조언에 따른
- Example: It would be ill-advised to invest all your savings in a single stock.

### self-deprecating
- Meaning: making fun of yourself; claiming to be less skilled than you really are
- Korean: 자기 비하적인, 자신을 낮추는
- Example: The British are famously self-deprecating; take their modesty with a pinch of salt.

### squander
- Meaning: to waste something that could have been put to good use
- Korean: 낭비하다, 허비하다
- Example: Don't squander your money on textbooks — save it until you can buy dozens of doughnuts.

### mellifluous
- Meaning: smooth, sweet, and pleasant to listen to (especially a voice)
- Korean: 부드럽고 감미로운
- Example: Nighy attributes the podcast's success to his mellifluous voice.

### refer to ~ as ~
- Meaning: to call or describe something/someone as something
- Korean: ~을 ~라고 부르다
- Example: I refer to Sue as "Queen of EBS Radio" even though she hates it.

### at ease
- Meaning: comfortable, relaxed; not anxious
- Korean: 편안한, 마음이 놓이는
- Example: Sue almost always seems at ease when she's on the radio.

### spontaneity
- Meaning: the quality of being able to do things immediately without planning
- Korean: 즉흥성, 자발성
- Example: Bill Nighy's spontaneity is what makes the podcast so entertaining.

### stand one's ground
- Meaning: to refuse to change your mind or back down
- Korean: 자기 입장을 고수하다
- Example: I'm standing my ground when it comes to generative AI.

### tick / check all the right boxes
- Meaning: to fulfill all the criteria or expectations
- Korean: 모든 조건을 만족시키다
- Example: Sue, your resume ticked all the right boxes. We think you're perfect for the job.

### respectable
- Meaning: dressed smartly and looking socially acceptable
- Korean: 단정한, 깔끔한
- Example: I'm not very good at looking respectable. The only suit I own doesn't fit me.

## 5. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. **self-deprecating** — making fun of yourself (자기 비하적인)
2. **squander** — to waste something valuable (낭비하다)
3. **stand one's ground** — to refuse to change your mind (자기 입장을 고수하다)
4. **tick all the right boxes** — to fulfill all the criteria (모든 조건을 만족시키다)
5. **respectable** — dressed smartly; looking socially acceptable (단정한)
