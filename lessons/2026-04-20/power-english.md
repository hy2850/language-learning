# Trying New Cuisines — I'm Up for Anything

## 1. Warm-up

Today's episode is about adventurous eating. The hosts chat about their weekends before introducing a dialogue where two friends decide to try Moroccan food for the first time on a weeknight. The theme is being open to new food experiences and the rewards of stepping outside your comfort zone.

오늘 에피소드는 새로운 음식에 도전하는 것에 관한 내용입니다. 진행자들이 주말 이야기를 나눈 후, 두 친구가 평일 저녁에 모로코 음식을 처음 먹어보는 다이얼로그를 소개합니다. 낯선 음식에 열린 마음을 갖는 것, 그리고 편안한 영역을 벗어났을 때 얻는 기쁨이 주제입니다.

---

## 2. Expressions

**1. be up for (something)**
- To be willing and open to doing something that is suggested; a relaxed "sure, why not" attitude.
- 제안받은 것을 기꺼이 해보려는 마음이 있다, 할 의향이 있다

**2. take a risk**
- To do something uncertain — it might not work out, but the possible reward is worth trying.
- 결과가 불확실하더라도 도전해보다, 위험을 감수하다

**3. discover hidden gems**
- To find something wonderful that is not well-known or obvious; a treasure most people have missed.
- 숨겨진 보석 같은 장소나 것을 발견하다, 잘 알려지지 않은 훌륭한 것을 찾아내다

## 5. Notes

### be up for (something)
- To be open and willing to do something when it's suggested. It's not necessarily strong desire — more of a "sure, why not" attitude. Can also ask about physical ability to do something.
- **A:** We're thinking of going rock climbing this weekend. Are you up for it?
  **B:** Rock climbing? Sure, I'm up for it. Never tried it before.
- He said he was up for anything as long as it was fun.

### take a risk
- To do something where the outcome is uncertain — you might fail, but you try anyway because the potential reward is worth it.
- **A:** This dish sounds really unusual. Should we order it?
  **B:** Let's take a risk. That's how you find new favorites.
- Sometimes you have to take a risk to succeed.

### discover hidden gems
- To find something wonderful — a restaurant, a place, a thing — that most people don't know about. The "gem" is valuable but hidden from view.
- **A:** I found the most amazing little cafe in that alley by the station.
  **B:** No way! I walked past there a hundred times. What a hidden gem.
- That tiny bookshop turned out to be a hidden gem — rare books at unbelievable prices.

---

## 6. Power Pattern

### At least we can say...
- Used when a result is uncertain or didn't go as hoped. It shifts focus from the outcome to the action itself — you can be proud of what you did, regardless of how it turned out.
- 결과가 어떻든 간에, 적어도 우리가 ~했다고 말할 수 있다 (행동 자체에 의미를 두는 표현)

**Examples:**
1. Even though we had to stop early, at least we can say we went hiking today.
2. Our team may have lost badly, but at least we can say we played our hardest.

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### locals
- People who live in a specific area and know it well; not tourists. Can include long-term foreign residents who know the area deeply.
- 현지인, 그 지역에 사는 사람들 (오래 거주한 외국인도 포함될 수 있음)

**Examples:**
1. This place feels like one of those spots locals know about, but tourists miss.
2. Ask the locals — they always know the best places to eat.
