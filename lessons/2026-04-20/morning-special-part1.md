## 2. Today's Reckless Sentence (오늘의 무모한 문장)

### Prompt

- **Korean:** 사건이 일파만파로 번지고 있다
- **Intent / nuance:** Something is spreading and escalating rapidly and uncontrollably in all directions — like a ripple or wave effect that cannot be stopped. Inspired by today's discussion about how the Middle East conflict is affecting everything from gas stations and grocery stores to neighborhood dry cleaners.

### Best Listener Answers

1. **정영진:** "It spreads all around everywhere." → Host note: "It's spreading all around, everywhere" (progressive -ing sounds more natural)
2. **김희원:** "The case is spreading like a germ." (excellent use of simile)
3. **영이미(Young'imi):** "It's spreading like wildfire." (common English expression for rapid spread)
4. **천희란:** "The incident is having a massive ripple effect." (note: add "everyone/everything" after "affecting" for grammatical completeness: "It's affecting everyone everywhere")
5. **Joyful Union:** "It's spreading like crazy, getting hazy." (poetic/lyrical — hosts praised its rhyme)
6. **해밀맨:** "It's like the butterfly effect."
7. **Another listener:** "The situation is snowballing."
8. **Kim Hyunchul (김현철):** "This is getting way out of hand." ← **Selected winner**
9. **Another listener:** "The event is causing a domino chain reaction. It's spreading unstoppably." (note: "domino effect" and "chain reaction" are two separate expressions; better used separately)

### Winners

| Listener | Answer | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| **김현철** | "This is getting way out of hand." | 시카 크림과 앰플 |
| **Joyful Union** | "It's spreading like crazy, getting hazy." | 단백질 쉐이크 (praised for poetic rhyme) |

### Host Suggestions / Endorsed Lines

- **Shane:** "The situation is spiraling out of control." (spiral = 빙글빙글 돌면서 걷잡을 수 없이 움직이는 이미지)
- **Sue:** "Things are getting out of hand."

### Nuance Notes

- **일파만파 (ripple effect)** maps well to: *spreading like wildfire / snowballing / ripple effect / out of hand / out of control / spiraling*
- Avoid combining two separate idioms in one phrase (e.g., "domino chain reaction" — use one or the other)
- When the subject is vague, use "it," "the situation," "the event," or "the incident" as the subject

## 3. Five Headlines

### Headline 1 — Seoul Ozone Advisory

- **Topic:** Seoul's first citywide ozone advisory of the year
- **Main sentence:** The capital issued its first citywide ozone advisory of the year on Sunday, after hourly concentrations in some areas, including Seodaemun District, exceeded the 0.12 ppm threshold.
- **Korean summary:** 서울시가 지난 일요일 서대문구 등 일부 지역에서 시간당 오존 농도가 기준치인 0.12ppm을 초과하자 올해 첫 오존주의보를 전 도시에 발령했습니다.
- **Useful vocabulary:**
  - **citywide**
    - meaning: affecting the entire city
    - Korean: 전 도시에 걸친
  - **hourly concentration**
    - meaning: the amount of a substance (e.g., a pollutant) measured per hour
    - Korean: 시간당 농도
  - **threshold**
    - meaning: the level at which an advisory is triggered
    - Korean: 기준치
  - **secondary pollutant**
    - meaning: a pollutant formed by chemical reactions in the atmosphere (ozone = VOCs + nitrogen oxides + sunlight)
    - Korean: 2차 오염물질
  - **nationwide** (contrast)
    - meaning: affecting the entire country
    - Korean: 전국적인 (cf. citywide = 시 전역에 걸친)
- **Useful sentences:**
  - "A citywide bus driver strike has disrupted public transportation services." (from recap)
    - Korean: 전 도시에 걸친 버스 파업이 대중교통 서비스를 마비시켰습니다.
- **Note:** Even if only one of Seoul's 25 districts exceeds the threshold, the advisory is issued city-wide.

### Headline 2 — FSS Virtual Account Consumer Alert

- **Topic:** Financial Supervisory Service warns of virtual account fraud
- **Main sentence:** The Financial Supervisory Service has issued a consumer alert regarding virtual accounts, warning that these temporary verification tools for bill payments and online shopping are being increasingly exploited for criminal scams.
- **Korean summary:** 금융감독원(FSS)이 대금 결제 및 온라인 쇼핑에 쓰이는 가상계좌가 범죄에 악용되는 사례가 늘고 있다며 소비자 경보를 발령했습니다.
- **Useful vocabulary:**
  - **virtual account (가상계좌)**
    - meaning: a one-time temporary account number used for bill payments or online shopping
    - Korean: 가상계좌 (일회성 입금 전용 계좌)
  - **verification tool**
    - meaning: a method or system to check whether something is true or authentic
    - Korean: 검증·확인 도구
  - **exploit**
    - meaning: to take advantage of something unfairly for one's own gain; to misuse
    - Korean: 악용하다
  - **FSS (Financial Supervisory Service)**
    - Korean: 금융감독원
- **Useful sentences:**
  - "These virtual accounts can be exploited for criminal scams."
    - Korean: 이 가상계좌들은 범죄 사기에 악용될 수 있습니다.
  - "Experts warn that personal data can be exploited if not properly protected." (from recap)
    - Korean: 전문가들은 개인정보가 제대로 보호되지 않으면 악용될 수 있다고 경고합니다.
- **Note:** Scammers buy virtual accounts under other people's names to launder money or disguise themselves as legitimate businesses.

### Headline 3 — K-Hygiene

- **Topic:** British media coins the term "K-hygiene" for Korea's cleanliness culture
- **Main sentence:** A British media outlet has highlighted Korea's clean lifestyle culture, coining the term "K-hygiene" to describe an advanced cleanliness system that integrates cutting-edge technology with meticulous daily habits.
- **Korean summary:** 영국의 한 언론(데일리 메일)이 한국의 청결 생활 문화를 조명하며 '케이 위생(K-hygiene)'이라는 표현을 만들어 첨단 기술과 꼼꼼한 일상 습관이 결합한 체계로 소개했습니다.
- **Useful vocabulary:**
  - **coin (a term)**
    - meaning: to invent or create a new word or expression
    - Korean: (새 표현을) 만들다, 창안하다
    - Note: Say "a term has been coined" or "a newly coined term" — not "making" a term
  - **meticulous**
    - meaning: very careful, paying close attention to small details; leaving no gaps
    - Korean: 꼼꼼한, 빈틈없는
  - **daily habits**
    - meaning: what we do every day for health or productivity
    - Korean: 일상 생활 습관
  - **spotless**
    - meaning: extremely clean; not even a speck of dust
    - Korean: 먼지 한 톨 없이 깨끗한
- **Useful sentences:**
  - "The teen development program encourages students to create meticulous daily habits." (from recap)
    - Korean: 청소년 개발 프로그램은 학생들이 꼼꼼한 일상 습관을 만들도록 장려합니다.
  - "The Seoul subway system is spotless."
    - Korean: 서울 지하철은 먼지 한 톨 없이 깨끗합니다.
- **Note:** The article also cites Korea's waste separation system, food waste management (종량제 봉투), and the custom of removing shoes indoors as key reasons.

### Headline 4 — Chinese Humanoid Robots Half Marathon

- **Topic:** Chinese humanoid robots complete half marathon with record performance
- **Main sentence:** Dozens of Chinese-made humanoid robots demonstrated rapidly advancing athleticism during a half marathon on Sunday, with the winning model finishing in 50 minutes and 26 seconds, marking a significant leap from the lackluster performances seen just a year ago.
- **Korean summary:** 중국제 휴머노이드 로봇들이 일요일 베이징 하프마라톤에서 빠르게 향상된 운동 능력을 선보였으며, 우승 로봇 '샨디엔(Lightning)'이 50분 26초로 완주해 작년의 부진한 성적 대비 큰 도약을 이뤄냈습니다.
- **Useful vocabulary:**
  - **leap**
    - meaning: a major progress or big rapid advancement (literally: to jump)
    - Korean: 큰 도약, 큰 진전
  - **lackluster**
    - meaning: unimpressive, failing to meet expectations; dull
    - Korean: 부진한, 활기 없는, 윤기 없는
  - **humanoid**
    - meaning: a robot designed to resemble the human form
    - Korean: 인간의 모습을 비슷하게 흉내 낸 (로봇)
- **Useful sentences:**
  - "One small step for man, one giant leap for mankind." (Neil Armstrong — illustrates literal + metaphorical use of leap)
    - Korean: 인간에게는 작은 한 걸음이지만, 인류에게는 위대한 도약입니다.
  - "Last year we saw some lackluster performances, but this year there was a huge leap in the technology."
    - Korean: 작년에는 부진한 성적을 보였지만, 올해는 기술 면에서 엄청난 도약이 있었습니다.
  - "The candidate's lackluster debate performance raised concerns about his qualifications." (from recap)
    - Korean: 그 후보의 부진한 토론 성적은 그의 자질에 대한 우려를 낳았습니다.
- **Note:** Human half-marathon world record is 57 min 20 sec. The winning robot "Shandiyan (Lightning/번개)" finished in 50 min 26 sec — faster than the human record. Participating teams jumped fivefold from 20 to 100. Remote-controlled robots had their finishing times multiplied by 1.2 to determine actual ranking.

### Headline 5 — Titanic Life Jacket Auction

- **Topic:** Titanic first-class survivor's life jacket sells at record auction price
- **Main sentence:** A life jacket worn by a first-class survivor of the Titanic sold at auction for 670,000 pounds, or about 1.3 billion won, marking the only time in the 114 years since the ship sank that such an artifact has been offered for sale.
- **Korean summary:** 타이타닉 1등석 생존자가 실제로 착용했던 구명조끼가 경매에서 67만 파운드(약 13억 원)에 낙찰되었으며, 이는 침몰 이후 114년 역사상 관련 구명조끼가 판매에 나온 최초의 사례입니다.
- **Useful vocabulary:**
  - **artifact**
    - meaning: a historical object of cultural or archaeological significance
    - Korean: 유물
  - **such an artifact**
    - meaning: this kind of artifact (referring back to something just mentioned in context)
    - Korean: 이러한 유물, 그러한 유물
    - Note: *such* here = "this kind of" (not the exclamatory "such a lovely day")
  - **be offered for sale**
    - meaning: to be made available for purchase
    - Korean: 판매에 내놓다, 판매 가능 상태가 되다
    - Note: In Western countries, "For Sale" signs appear on houses for sale; "For Rent" / "To Let" (UK) for rentals
- **Useful sentences:**
  - "This marks the only time in 114 years since the ship sank that such an artifact has been offered for sale."
    - Korean: 이는 타이타닉 침몰 이후 114년 만에 이러한 유물이 판매에 나온 유일한 사례입니다.
  - "Officials warn that such a delay could disrupt operations at the plant." (from recap)
    - Korean: 관계자들은 이러한 지체가 공장 운영을 방해할 수 있다고 경고합니다.
- **Note:** The life jacket was owned by first-class passenger Laura Mabel Francatelli, who survived in 1912. Only 12 of the original ~3,500 life jackets still exist. Previous auction record for Titanic memorabilia was £390,000.

## 4. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. **citywide**
   - meaning: affecting the entire city
   - example: "A citywide bus driver strike has disrupted public transportation services."
   - Korean: 전 도시에 걸친 버스 파업이 대중교통 서비스를 마비시켰습니다.

2. **exploit**
   - meaning: to misuse or take unfair advantage of something (악용하다)
   - example: "Experts warn that personal data can be exploited if not properly protected."
   - Korean: 전문가들은 개인정보가 제대로 보호되지 않으면 악용될 수 있다고 경고합니다.

3. **meticulous daily habits**
   - meaning: very careful, detail-oriented everyday routines (꼼꼼한 일상 습관)
   - example: "The teen development program encourages students to create meticulous daily habits."
   - Korean: 청소년 개발 프로그램은 학생들이 꼼꼼한 일상 습관을 형성하도록 장려합니다.

4. **lackluster**
   - meaning: unimpressive, below expectations (부진한)
   - example: "The candidate's lackluster debate performance raised concerns about his qualifications."
   - Korean: 그 후보의 부진한 토론 성적은 그의 자질에 대한 우려를 낳았습니다.

5. **such a(n) [something]**
   - meaning: this kind of / that kind of [noun] (이러한, 그러한)
   - example: "Officials warn that such a delay could disrupt operations at the plant."
   - Korean: 관계자들은 이러한 지체가 공장 운영을 방해할 수 있다고 경고합니다.
