## 2. Today's Idiom
- Idiom: a flash in the pan
- Korean meaning: 반짝 유행, 잠깐 인기를 끌거나 관심을 받았지만 오래가지 않는 것

### Setup
- 백화점에서 혼수용품을 둘러보며 Jason의 친구 Roberto에게 줄 결혼 선물을 고르는 상황.
- 두 사람은 침구, 가전제품, 고급 블렌더 등을 보다가 Roberto의 예전 건강/웰빙 관심사가 오래가지 않았다는 이야기를 나눔.

### Dialogue
**Chesu:** I never thought Roberto would settle down so quickly. I honestly thought he had, I don't know, five more years of traveling, having adventures and doing volunteer work left in him. What kind of person is Roberto's bride anyway?

**Jason:** Oh, she's very down to earth. They actually met five years ago.

**Chesu:** Oh wow! That long then? How come you're buying a physical gift? You could just go Korean style and give them a convenient cash gift at the wedding.

**Jason:** Yeah, you know I thought about doing that, but he told me that his parents are coming all the way from South America for the wedding and they're very traditional. And you know in Latin culture, physical gifts are highly preferred over cash; cash is seen as a little too impersonal. So he asked guests who are from overseas to abstain from cash and go for traditional gifts instead.

**Chesu:** Oh, okay, I get it. Hey, look at this bedding set. It's absolutely gorgeous! Oh, and it feels so incredibly soft.

**Jason:** Yeah, it looks okay, but I don't know. There are definitely too many flowers on it.

**Chesu:** How about that one over there?

**Jason:** Oh, that looks really sophisticated. Yeah, but that's not cotton; they prefer natural fabrics.

**Chesu:** Okay then, let's check the appliances section. By the way, when are the groom's parents arriving?

**Jason:** Next week. They will have like two weeks before the wedding to look around and tour the city. I promised him I will kind of help them look around and introduce them to some good restaurants and the local museum.

**Chesu:** Well, that sounds really nice. Hey, look at this dishwasher! It's the latest model and it's currently on sale.

**Jason:** Oh, it actually looks great. That could be a really useful option. Oh wait, no, never mind. It's only for built-in counters, and if I remember correctly, they are renting their current place.

**Chesu:** Ah, wait, wait, Jason. I found the absolute perfect gift. Look at this! It's the latest blender from MS Electronics. It has all these amazing functions, and it even comes with two spare sets of blades.

**Jason:** Hmm... I don't know about that.

**Chesu:** Oh come on! It's the perfect gift for Roberto. Didn't you tell me before that he was really into well-being and nutrition? A high-end blender is the ultimate tool for health-conscious people.

**Jason:** Oh yeah, that is completely over. He rarely even goes hiking on the weekends anymore.

**Chesu:** Oh really? He gave up on all of that?

**Jason:** Yeah, his health and nutrition phase was just a flash in the pan. Now he's much more into computer games.

**Chesu:** Oh, I see. Then how about this LED mask? It's pricey, but it's right at the top of every newlyweds' wish list.

### Meaning
- A flash in the pan describes something that is very momentary.
- It can refer to a trend, thought, interest, or phase that was hot for a short time but did not last.
- It can also describe a person who became popular or successful briefly and then disappeared from public attention.

### Host Explanation Notes
- Roberto's health and nutrition phase was used as the main example: he used to be into well-being, but that interest is over now.
- The hosts explained that people can also be called a flash in the pan, such as a singer popular for one year or an athlete good for only one season.
- The key nuance is lack of staying power: brief popularity, brief success, or a short-lived phase.

### Example Usages
- His health and nutrition phase was just a flash in the pan.
- That singer was a flash in the pan.
- That actor was a flash in the pan.

### Similar Expressions
- one-hit wonder
- short-lived phase
- short-lived trend

## 4. Vocabulary and Useful Sentences

### juggernaut
- Meaning: something powerful that is already moving and feels impossible to stop
- Korean: 거침없이 나아가는 강력한 존재, 막기 힘든 강호
- Useful sentence: Cape Verde faces Argentina's World Cup juggernaut; its fans aren't stressed.
- Sentence Korean: 카보베르데가 월드컵의 강호 아르헨티나와 맞붙지만 팬들은 전혀 긴장하지 않는다.
- Note: 스포츠 기사에서 압도적인 강팀이나 막기 어려운 흐름을 표현할 때 유용함.

### upset
- Meaning: an unexpected result, especially when the underdog beats the favorite; as an adjective, it means angry or emotionally distressed
- Korean: 이변, 뜻밖의 승리; 속상한
- Useful sentence: It would surely be one of the biggest upsets in the competition's history.
- Sentence Korean: 그것은 대회 역사상 가장 큰 이변 중 하나가 될 것이 분명했다.
- Note: 명사 upset은 스포츠에서 "이변"이라는 뜻이고, 형용사 upset은 "속상한"이라는 뜻으로 쓰임.

### the pressure is on
- Meaning: a situation has become tense because expectations or stakes are high
- Korean: 부담감이 크다, 압박이 걸려 있다
- Useful sentence: The pressure is on, but on the streets of Praia, any suggestion of stress meets a shrug and a chuckle.
- Sentence Korean: 부담감은 크지만 프라이아 거리에서 스트레스를 언급하면 사람들은 그저 어깨를 으쓱하며 웃어넘긴다.
- Note: 경기, 시험, 발표처럼 결과에 대한 부담이 커진 상황에서 쓸 수 있음.

### a way of life
- Meaning: the typical way people live or the usual attitude of a place or group
- Korean: 삶의 방식
- Useful sentence: Here on this tiny archipelago of about five hundred thousand people, no stress is a way of life.
- Sentence Korean: 약 50만 명이 사는 이 작은 군도에서 "no stress"는 삶의 방식이다.
- Note: 단순한 습관보다 한 사회나 집단에 깊게 자리 잡은 생활 방식이나 분위기를 말함.

### jersey
- Meaning: a sports uniform shirt worn by players or fans
- Korean: 운동 경기용 유니폼 상의
- Useful sentence: It is printed on souvenir T-shirts and football jerseys lining shop windows.
- Sentence Korean: 그 문구는 상점 창가에 진열된 기념 티셔츠와 축구 유니폼에도 인쇄되어 있다.
- Note: 축구, 야구, 농구 등 여러 종목의 유니폼 상의를 말할 때 자연스럽게 쓰는 단어.

### a run of successes
- Meaning: a series or group of successful results, not necessarily perfectly consecutive
- Korean: 일련의 성공, 성공 행진
- Useful sentence: That debut for Cape Verde set off a run of successes.
- Sentence Korean: 카보베르데의 그 데뷔는 이어지는 성공 행진의 신호탄이 되었다.
- Note: consecutive처럼 반드시 빈틈없이 이어진다는 뜻은 아니고, 성공이 여러 차례 이어지는 흐름을 말함.

### pull something off
- Meaning: to succeed in doing something difficult or unexpected
- Korean: 어려운 일을 해내다, 성사시키다
- Useful sentence: Nobody believed he could pull off the trick, but he did.
- Sentence Korean: 아무도 그가 그 묘기를 해낼 수 있을 거라고 믿지 않았지만, 그는 해냈다.
- Note: 기대하기 어려운 일이나 난도가 높은 일을 성공했을 때 쓰는 표현.

### fixture
- Meaning: someone or something that is regularly present and feels almost always there
- Korean: 늘 보이는 존재, 터줏대감; 붙박이 설비
- Useful sentence: It pulled off a sensational World Cup qualification, defeating the likes of Cameroon, a fixture at World Cups for decades.
- Sentence Korean: 카보베르데는 수십 년 동안 월드컵에서 꾸준히 볼 수 있었던 카메룬 같은 강호들을 꺾고 놀라운 월드컵 본선 진출을 이루어냈다.
- Note: 집의 light fixture처럼 고정된 물건에도 쓰이고, 스포츠에서는 계속 등장하는 팀이나 인물에도 씀.

### pull something up
- Meaning: to bring up a file, photo, or document so someone can see it
- Korean: 파일이나 사진을 불러오다, 띄우다
- Useful sentence: I'll pull the files up, but it's going to take some time.
- Sentence Korean: 파일을 불러오겠습니다. 다만 시간이 좀 걸릴 겁니다.
- Note: 휴대폰이나 컴퓨터에서 사진, 문서, 자료를 찾아 화면에 띄울 때 자주 쓰임.

### draw on something
- Meaning: to use part of a larger resource, knowledge, experience, or group
- Korean: 활용하다, 이용하다, 끌어오다
- Useful sentence: He drew on his knowledge of geography to answer the question correctly.
- Sentence Korean: 그는 지리 상식을 활용해서 그 질문에 정확히 답했다.
- Note: draw on experience, draw on memory처럼 자신이 가진 지식이나 경험을 활용한다는 뜻으로도 많이 씀.

### alumni
- Meaning: people who attended or graduated from a school or program
- Korean: 졸업생, 동문
- Useful sentence: After gaining fame for his success, he suddenly became popular at his school alumni gatherings.
- Sentence Korean: 성공으로 유명해진 뒤 그는 학교 동문 모임에서 갑자기 인기를 얻게 되었다.
- Note: 학교나 교육 프로그램 출신자를 말할 때 많이 쓰며, 방송에서는 EPIF 출신 선수들을 설명할 때 사용됨.

## 5. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. upset
- meaning: an unexpected sports result where the underdog beats the favorite; also a verb meaning to defeat unexpectedly
- example: They upset the defending champions in the semifinals.
- Korean: 이변, 이변을 일으켜 꺾다

2. pull something off
- meaning: to complete or succeed in something difficult or unexpected
- example: Nobody believed he could pull off the trick, but he did.
- Korean: 어려운 일을 해내다, 성사시키다

3. pull something up
- meaning: to bring up a file, photo, or document for viewing
- example: I'll pull the files up, but it's going to take some time.
- Korean: 파일이나 사진을 불러오다, 띄우다

4. draw on something
- meaning: to use part of a larger resource, experience, or knowledge
- example: He drew on his knowledge of geography to answer the question correctly.
- Korean: 활용하다, 이용하다

5. alumni
- meaning: people who attended or graduated from a school or program
- example: After gaining fame for his success, he suddenly became popular at his school alumni gatherings.
- Korean: 졸업생, 동문
