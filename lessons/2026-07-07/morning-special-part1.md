## 2. Today's Reckless Sentence (오늘의 무모한 문장)

### Prompt
- Korean: 걔는 다 차려진 밥상에 숟가락만 얹었어.
- Intent / nuance: 다른 사람이 거의 다 해 놓은 일에 별다른 노력 없이 끼어들어 쉽게 이득이나 공을 얻으려는 뉘앙스. 직역하면 영어권 청자가 이해하기 어려울 수 있어, "노력 없이 얻다", "남의 공에 편승하다", "마지막에 살짝 얹다" 같은 의미 중심 표현이 더 자연스러움.

### Best Listener Answers
1. She gets things without having to work for them.
2. He is an absolute free rider.
3. He's just riding on his coattails.
4. She just joined in without any chipping in.
5. It's like I put my little stone on a mountain.
6. I just put little cherry on top.

### Winner / Selected Answer
- Winner: 9021 - She gets things without having to work for them.
- Winner: 고유석 - I just put little cherry on top.
- Why it works: 첫 번째 문장은 노력 없이 무언가를 얻는다는 비판적 느낌이 분명해서 Paul이 "very creative"라고 하며 선택함. 두 번째 문장은 "cherry on top"이라는 영어권 표현을 활용해, 마지막에 작은 것만 더한 느낌을 원어민이 쉽게 이해할 수 있다고 설명되며 선택됨.

### Host Suggestions / Endorsed Lines
- He's just reached the finish line without suffering hardship.
- He's just riding on other people's coattails.
- She just joined without chipping anything in.
- We were almost done, but she just pitched in a spoon. [literal translation; hosts noted it needs context]
- It's like I put my little stone on a mountain and said, "I built it."
- I just put a little cherry on top.
- I only need to add the finishing touches.
- I don't deserve all the spotlight.
- He or she doesn't deserve all the spotlight.

## 3. Five Headlines

### Headline 1
- Topic: Enhanced identity verification for mobile phone sign-ups
- Main sentence: Korea's major mobile carriers and mobile virtual network operators have introduced enhanced multi-factor identity verification across all sales channels, meaning that a physical ID card alone is no longer sufficient to sign up for a new mobile plan or switch carriers.
- Korean summary: 국내 이동통신사와 알뜰폰 사업자들이 신규 가입이나 번호 이동 때 신분증만으로는 부족한 강화된 다중 인증 절차를 적용하기 시작했다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - mobile carrier
    - meaning: a company that provides mobile phone service
    - Korean: 이동통신사
  - mobile virtual network operator / MVNO
    - meaning: a mobile service provider known in Korean as an "알뜰폰" operator
    - Korean: 알뜰폰 사업자
  - enhanced
    - meaning: made better, stronger, or greater than before
    - Korean: 강화된 / 개선된
  - ID verification
    - meaning: the process of checking someone's identity
    - Korean: 본인 확인 절차
  - no longer sufficient
    - meaning: not enough anymore
    - Korean: 더 이상 충분하지 않은 / 이제 부족한
  - mobile plan
    - meaning: a cell phone service plan or rate plan
    - Korean: 휴대폰 요금제
- Useful sentences:
  - ID cards alone are no longer sufficient.
    - Korean: 신분증만으로는 더 이상 충분하지 않습니다.
  - What plan are you on?
    - Korean: 무슨 휴대폰 요금제를 쓰고 있어요?
  - Always check up on the latest visa requirements when traveling, as the previously requested documents and forms may no longer be sufficient.
    - Korean: 여행할 때는 최신 비자 요건을 꼭 확인하세요. 예전에 요구되던 서류만으로는 이제 부족할 수 있습니다.
- Note: no longer sufficient는 규정이나 요구 조건이 바뀌어 "예전 방식만으로는 부족하다"는 뜻을 전할 때 유용함.

### Headline 2
- Topic: Samgyetang prices rise in Seoul
- Main sentence: The average price of a bowl of samgyetang in Seoul has surged by 29 percent over the past five years, reaching more than 18,000 won as of May 2026, according to the Korea Consumer Agency.
- Korean summary: 서울 지역 삼계탕 평균 가격이 최근 5년 동안 29% 올라 2026년 5월 기준 18,000원을 넘어섰다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - a bowl of
    - meaning: a portion or serving of food, especially soup, stew, or rice
    - Korean: ~ 한 그릇
  - as of
    - meaning: from a certain date or based on a certain point in time
    - Korean: ~ 기준으로 / ~일부로
- Useful sentences:
  - We would rather say "a bowl of soup" or "a bowl of samgyetang."
    - Korean: 영어에서는 "수프 한 그릇", "삼계탕 한 그릇"처럼 말하는 것이 더 자연스럽습니다.
  - If you're feeling under the weather, a bowl of soup is bound to perk you up.
    - Korean: 몸이 좀 안 좋을 때는 따뜻한 수프 한 그릇이 기운을 나게 해 줄 거예요.
- Note: soup나 stew처럼 셀 수 없는 음식은 one soup보다 a bowl of soup처럼 양과 그릇을 함께 말하는 것이 자연스러움.

### Headline 3
- Topic: Wildfires sweep across southern Europe
- Main sentence: Raging wildfires, fueled by intense heat and dry air, are sweeping across southern Europe, devouring over 19,000 hectares of land across Portugal, Spain, France, and Greece, equivalent to more than 65 times the size of Yoido.
- Korean summary: 폭염과 건조한 공기로 남유럽 산불이 확산되며 포르투갈, 스페인, 프랑스, 그리스에서 여의도 65배가 넘는 면적이 불탔다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - devour
    - meaning: to eat quickly and energetically; for fire, to consume or destroy aggressively
    - Korean: 집어삼키다 / 맹렬히 먹어 치우다
  - equivalent to
    - meaning: the same as or very similar to, often used to make a large number easier to picture
    - Korean: ~와 같은 / ~에 해당하는
- Useful sentences:
  - Wildfires are devouring a lot of land in Europe.
    - Korean: 유럽에서 산불이 많은 지역을 집어삼키고 있습니다.
  - The donkey devoured a dozen delicious doughnuts in under a minute.
    - Korean: 당나귀가 1분도 안 돼서 맛있는 도넛 열두 개를 먹어 치웠습니다.
- Note: devour는 단순한 destroy보다 의도성과 공격성이 느껴지는 더 강한 이미지의 동사로, 산불 같은 재난 기사에 자주 어울림.

### Headline 4
- Topic: Japan demonstrates planetary defense technology
- Main sentence: Japan has successfully demonstrated a key planetary defense collision prevention technology, according to local media, becoming the second nation after the U.S. to secure this capability despite lagging in general rocket technology.
- Korean summary: 일본이 소행성과 지구의 충돌을 막는 지구 방어 핵심 기술을 실증해, 로켓 기술 전반에서는 뒤처졌음에도 미국에 이어 두 번째로 해당 능력을 확보했다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - collision
    - meaning: when something crashes into or hits something else
    - Korean: 충돌
  - prevention
    - meaning: stopping something from happening
    - Korean: 예방 / 방지
  - collision prevention technology
    - meaning: technology that stops things from crashing into each other
    - Korean: 충돌 방지 기술
  - lag / lagging in
    - meaning: to go more slowly than others or fall behind in a field
    - Korean: 뒤처지다
- Useful sentences:
  - Japan is lagging in general rocket technology; however, when it comes to planetary defense, it's only the second country in the world to have this technology.
    - Korean: 일본은 로켓 기술 전반에서는 뒤처져 있지만, 지구 방어 분야에서는 이 기술을 보유한 세계 두 번째 국가입니다.
  - Hurry up, Sue. We're lagging behind the others. You don't want to get left behind, do you?
    - Korean: 서둘러, Sue. 우리 다른 사람들보다 뒤처지고 있어. 혼자 뒤에 남고 싶지는 않잖아?
- Note: lag는 인터넷 영상이 늦어질 때도 쓰이고, 특정 기술이나 분야에서 뒤처진다는 의미로도 확장됨.

### Headline 5
- Topic: Social isolation may change the brain
- Main sentence: A Korean research team has found that even a single day of social isolation can change the brain in ways that make individuals less inclined to engage with new people, while identifying potential new therapeutic targets for depression and anxiety disorders.
- Korean summary: 한국 연구진은 단 하루의 사회적 고립만으로도 새로운 사람과 어울리려는 경향이 줄도록 뇌가 바뀔 수 있으며, 우울증과 불안 장애 치료의 새로운 표적도 찾을 수 있다고 밝혔다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - social isolation
    - meaning: being separated from social contact with other people
    - Korean: 사회적 고립
  - be less inclined to do something
    - meaning: to have less desire or willingness to do something
    - Korean: ~하려는 마음이 줄어들다 / ~하려는 경향이 줄다
  - therapeutic
    - meaning: related to therapy, healing, or treatment
    - Korean: 치료의 / 치료와 관련된
  - therapeutic target
    - meaning: a target for treatment that may help patients get better
    - Korean: 치료 표적
- Useful sentences:
  - People who are socially isolated are less inclined to meet new people.
    - Korean: 사회적으로 고립된 사람들은 새로운 사람을 만나려는 마음이 줄어듭니다.
  - After a hard day's work, I'm less inclined to socialize. I just want to eat dinner, watch TV, and go to bed.
    - Korean: 하루 종일 열심히 일하고 나면 사람들을 별로 만나고 싶지 않습니다. 그냥 저녁 먹고 TV 보고 자고 싶어요.
- Note: be less inclined to do something은 행동 경향이나 의지를 부드럽게 설명할 때 쓰기 좋아 연구 기사와 일상 대화 모두에 활용 가능함.

## 4. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. no longer sufficient
- meaning: not enough anymore
- example: Always check up on the latest visa requirements when traveling, as the previously requested documents and forms may no longer be sufficient.
- Korean: 더 이상 충분하지 않다 / 부족하다

2. a bowl of something
- meaning: a portion or serving of food, especially soup, stew, or rice
- example: If you're feeling under the weather, a bowl of soup is bound to perk you up.
- Korean: ~ 한 그릇

3. devour
- meaning: to eat quickly and energetically; figuratively, to consume or destroy
- example: The donkey devoured a dozen delicious doughnuts in under a minute.
- Korean: 집어삼키다 / 먹어 치우다

4. lag / lag behind
- meaning: to move too slowly or fall behind others
- example: Hurry up, Sue. We're lagging behind the others. You don't want to get left behind, do you?
- Korean: 뒤처지다

5. be less inclined to do something
- meaning: to have less desire or willingness to do something
- example: After a hard day's work, I'm less inclined to socialize. I just want to eat dinner, watch TV, and go to bed.
- Korean: ~하려는 마음이 줄다 / ~하려는 경향이 줄다
