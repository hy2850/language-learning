# When the AC Gives Up: A Sleepless Night Without AC

## 1. Warm-up

Jake and Emma are stuck waiting for their air conditioner to be repaired during a hot, humid week. After one miserable night without AC, they are exhausted, sweaty, and desperate for any small thing that might help them sleep.

제이크와 엠마는 무더운 날씨에 에어컨 수리를 기다려야 하는 상황입니다. 에어컨 없이 하룻밤을 보내고 나니 둘 다 지치고 땀에 젖어서, 잠을 잘 수만 있다면 뭐든 해 보고 싶은 마음입니다.

---

## 2. Expressions

**1. toss and turn**
- To keep moving around in bed because you cannot sleep comfortably.
- 잠을 설치며 뒤척이다

**2. drenched in sweat**
- Completely covered or soaked with sweat.
- 땀에 흠뻑 젖은

**3. get through**
- To survive or endure a difficult situation until it is over.
- 힘든 시간을 버텨 내다

---

## 3. Power Dialogue

**Jake:** I barely slept last night. Just tossed and turned in this heat all night.
**Emma:** Same here. I woke up drenched in sweat. This is unbearable, honestly.
**Jake:** Should we get fans or sleep somewhere else temporarily?
**Emma:** I bought two fans this morning; they should help us get through the nights.
**Jake:** Anything helps at this point. I'm running on three hours of sleep.
**Emma:** Me too, and work was brutal today because I'm so exhausted.
**Jake:** One week of thishow are we supposed to survive?
**Emma:** Coffee! Lots and lots of coffee!

## 5. Notes

### toss and turn
- When you toss and turn, you move around in bed again and again because you cannot fall asleep or stay comfortable.
- **A:** Did you sleep okay before the big meeting?
  **B:** Not really. I tossed and turned for hours.
- I tossed and turned all night because the room was too hot.

### drenched in sweat
- If you are drenched in sweat, your body or clothes are very wet from sweating. It can happen because of heat, exercise, sickness, or fear.
- **A:** You look like you just ran a marathon.
  **B:** I know. I'm drenched in sweat from walking in this heat.
- After cleaning the apartment without AC, she was drenched in sweat.

### get through
- To get through something means to endure a difficult time or situation until it ends. It often suggests that the situation takes effort and patience.
- **A:** This week is so stressful.
  **B:** Hang in there. We'll get through it together.
- These fans should help us get through the summer nights.

---

## 6. Power Pattern

### at this point
- Used to mean "now," especially after a series of events, attempts, or changes has led to the current situation.
- 이쯤 되면, 지금 상황에서는, 이제 와서는

**Examples:**
1. I'm so tired at this point that I could sleep on the floor.
2. We've tried every repair shop at this point, so let's just wait.

---

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### brutal
- Very difficult, unpleasant, or exhausting. It is stronger than just "hard" and often suggests that something really wears you down.
- 너무 힘든, 혹독한

**Examples:**
1. Working all day after three hours of sleep was brutal.
2. The commute was brutal because the train had no air conditioning.
