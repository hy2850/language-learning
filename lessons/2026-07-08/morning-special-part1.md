## 2. Today's Reckless Sentence (오늘의 무모한 문장)

### Prompt
- Korean: 얼굴에 철판 깔고 일단 해야 돼.
- Intent / nuance: 외국어를 배울 때 실수나 남의 시선을 두려워하지 말고, 조금 뻔뻔하고 대담하게 일단 해 보라는 뜻. 직역보다는 "be bold", "be thick-skinned", "don't worry about sounding silly" 같은 자연스러운 격려형 표현이 어울림.

### Best Listener Answers
1. Don't care about what others think and just start.
2. Just go for it with a thick skin placed on your face.
3. Go ahead, don't be shy.
4. You can't be self-conscious; just do it.
5. You have to be shameless to do it.
6. Go ahead, slap on your titanium face.
7. Get out of your comfort zone.
8. Just try it; no one's perfect.

### Winner / Selected Answer
- Winner: 김경미 - "Go ahead, don't be shy."
- Winner: 전용태 - "Go for it with an iron mask."
- Why it works: "Go ahead, don't be shy"는 간단하고 격려의 뉘앙스가 자연스럽고, "Go for it with an iron mask"는 한국어의 "철판" 이미지를 재미있게 살린 창의적인 표현으로 선택됨.

### Host Suggestions / Endorsed Lines

- Be thick-skinned.

## 3. Five Headlines

### Headline 1
- Topic: Global investment banks raise Korea growth forecast
- Main sentence: Eight major global investment banks have raised their average 2026 economic growth forecast for Korea to three percent, with the upward revision largely attributed to record-breaking June semiconductor exports.
- Korean summary: 해외 주요 투자은행들이 6월 반도체 수출 호조를 반영해 한국의 2026년 경제 성장률 전망치를 평균 3%로 상향 조정했다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - upward revision:
    - meaning: an increase in a previously estimated value, forecast, or target
    - Korean: 상향 조정
  - attribute A to B:
    - meaning: to regard A as being caused by, created by, or coming from B
    - Korean: A의 원인을 B에 있다고 보다 / A를 B의 결과로 보다
- Useful sentences:
  - The temperature drop is being attributed to a cold front moving in from the north.
    - Korean: 기온이 뚝 떨어진 것은 북쪽에서 내려온 한랭전선 때문인 것으로 추정됩니다.
- Note: 경제 기사에서 forecast, revision, attribute는 전망치가 왜 바뀌었는지 설명할 때 자주 함께 쓰임.

### Headline 2
- Topic: First fully annotated English translation of Samguk Yusa
- Main sentence: The first fully annotated English translation of Samguk Yusa has been published by the University of Hawaii Press under the title Vestiges of the Three Kingdoms of Ancient Korea, capturing a monumental work that has profoundly shaped Korean historical and cultural identity.
- Korean summary: 한국의 역사와 문화 정체성에 큰 영향을 준 삼국유사의 첫 완전 주석 영문 번역본이 하와이대학교 출판부에서 출간됐다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - vestige:
    - meaning: a trace or remnant of something from the past
    - Korean: 흔적 / 자취
  - fully annotated:
    - meaning: supplied with explanatory notes, critical comments, or supplementary details
    - Korean: 주석이 자세히 달린
  - monumental:
    - meaning: massive in size or scope, or having lasting historical significance
    - Korean: 기념비적인 / 역사적으로 의미가 큰
- Useful sentences:
  - The scientists made a monumental contribution to the field of medicine.
    - Korean: 그 과학자는 의학계에 아주 기념비적인 기여를 했습니다.
- Note: annotated와 monumental은 학술서, 번역서, 역사적 업적을 설명할 때 유용한 표현.

### Headline 3
- Topic: Multilingualism and younger brain age
- Main sentence: A recent study finds that multilingual individuals tend to have a younger estimated brain age, with earlier second-language acquisition and higher proficiency leading to a slower brain aging process.
- Korean summary: 여러 언어를 구사하는 사람은 뇌 나이가 더 젊은 경향이 있고, 제2언어를 일찍 배우고 능숙할수록 뇌 노화가 더디다는 연구 결과가 나왔다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - multilingual:
    - meaning: able to use or involving multiple languages
    - Korean: 여러 언어를 구사하는 / 다중 언어의
  - second-language acquisition:
    - meaning: the process of learning an additional language after establishing a native tongue; also the field that studies this process
    - Korean: 제2언어 습득
  - proficiency:
    - meaning: the state of having great skill, competence, or expertise in a specific area or task
    - Korean: 능숙도 / 숙련도
- Useful sentences:
  - The job requires a high level of proficiency in written and spoken English.
    - Korean: 그 일은 영어 쓰기와 말하기를 높은 수준으로 능숙하게 할 수 있는 사람을 요구합니다.
- Note: proficiency는 언어 실력뿐 아니라 특정 기술이나 업무 능숙도를 말할 때도 쓸 수 있음.

## 4. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. attribute A to B
- meaning: to regard A as being caused by B, or to identify B as the source or origin of A
- example: The temperature drop is being attributed to a cold front moving in from the north.
- Korean: A의 원인을 B에 있다고 보다 / B 때문이다

2. monumental
- meaning: massive in size or scope, or having enduring historical significance
- example: The scientists made a monumental contribution to the field of medicine.
- Korean: 기념비적인 / 아주 의미 있는

3. proficiency
- meaning: the state of having great skill, competence, or expertise in a specific area or task
- example: The job requires a high level of proficiency in written and spoken English.
- Korean: 능숙도 / 숙련도

4. proceeds
- meaning: money or profit generated from a specific activity, event, or sale
- example: A portion of the book's proceeds is donated directly to environmental charities.
- Korean: 수익금

5. to stabilize
- meaning: to make something steady and prevent it from fluctuating or deteriorating
- example: The diplomatic talks helped stabilize relations between the two neighboring countries.
- Korean: 안정시키다
