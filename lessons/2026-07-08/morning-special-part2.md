## 2. Today's Idiom
- Idiom: Good fences make good neighbors.
- Korean meaning: 친할수록 예의를 지켜야 한다; 명확한 경계가 좋은 관계를 만든다.

### Setup
- 최수진이 새로 이사한 12층 아파트에 Karen이 집 구경과 저녁 식사를 위해 놀러 온 상황.
- 최수진은 새 이웃에게 피해를 주지 않기 위해 밤 8시 이후에는 TV를 볼 때 Bluetooth earphones를 사용한다.

### Dialogue
**Chesu:** Karen, would you like some more tea?

**Karen:** Oh no, thank you. Wow, your new place is beautiful. The view from this floor is absolutely incredible. You can see the whole city skyline from up here.

**Chesu:** Thanks. I'm glad you could finally make it over tonight. I'm packing up some of this pasta so you can take it home. I made too much food.

**Karen:** Oh, don't mind if I do. It was delicious. So how do you like living in this neighborhood so far?

**Chesu:** Honestly, it's great. There are so many fantastic restaurants just a block away. There's a cozy Italian bistro, a bakery, and we should try the sushi place downstairs next time.

**Karen:** I noticed that barbecue spot on my way into the lobby. The building itself looks a bit old from the outside, but inside it looks really well maintained.

**Chesu:** It is. Management keeps everything pristine. But the walls are a bit thin. I actually heard from a neighbor that a dispute broke out last month between apartments 802 and 902 over floor noise.

**Karen:** Oh no, that sounds awful. Living through a war over noise is a total nightmare.

**Chesu:** Yeah, it sounded pretty intense. But luckily, there are no families with children living on my floor or on the one right above me.

**Karen:** That's a huge relief. Speaking of drama, I just remembered a new TV drama, the one starring that actor from the car commercial.

**Chesu:** Oh really? I didn't know he had a new show. What's the drama called?

**Karen:** I can't remember the title, but let's search for it. Where is your TV remote control?

**Chesu:** Here you go. Let's see. Going to the latest releases section. Oh, there it is, right at the top.

**Karen:** Oh yeah, I think I read something about that online. Everyone's watching it lately. Have you started it yet?

**Chesu:** No, I haven't watched it yet.

**Karen:** Well, let's watch it right now.

**Chesu:** Great, let's do it. Hold on, we need these first.

**Karen:** What are those, Bluetooth earphones? Is your TV speaker broken?

**Chesu:** No, it's perfectly fine. But it's past 8 p.m., and things get really quiet around here. I don't want to cause a noise complaint. So after eight, I just listen to music or watch TV with these earphones on.

**Karen:** That is really considerate of you.

**Chesu:** Well, I'm in this new building and, you know what they say? Good fences make good neighbors.

**Karen:** That's actually a great idea. Shall we watch the show with some popcorn?

### Meaning
- 관계는 사람들이 명확한 경계를 세우고 서로의 사생활을 존중할 때 더 좋아진다는 뜻.
- 물리적 경계나 개인적 경계가 오해를 미리 막고, 서로의 독립성을 존중하는 태도가 오히려 관계를 더 가깝게 만든다는 뉘앙스.

### Host Explanation Notes
- 외국의 주택가에서는 집 사이에 fence가 있어 각자의 공간과 사생활을 구분한다.
- 아파트처럼 이웃과 가까이 사는 경우에는 소음, 생활 습관, 개인 공간을 의식하는 것이 일종의 경계를 지키는 행동이 된다.
- 영어권에서는 personal space가 중요해서 누군가와 어깨만 스쳐도 sorry라고 말하는 것이 자연스럽다.

### Example Usages
- In the dialogue, wearing Bluetooth earphones after 8 p.m. works like a modern invisible fence that protects the relationship with neighbors.
- When you live close to other people, being mindful of noise is one way to practice the idea that good fences make good neighbors.

### Similar Expressions
- Clear boundaries make clean relationships.
- Separate purses keep friends together.
- An open door invites a thief; a closed door keeps a friend.

## 3. New York Times Article
- Title: Most working parents say finding enough time is a challenge.
- Topic summary: 미국의 일하는 부모들이 일과 육아를 병행하면서 충분한 시간과 유연성을 확보하기 어려워한다는 내용의 기사.
- Why it matters: 맞벌이 부모의 시간 부족은 개인의 문제가 아니라 직장 구조, 육아 부담, 성 역할 변화, 고용 안정성과 연결된 사회적 문제임을 보여준다.

### Key Article Passages

2. Sixty percent of those who do say they spend too little time with their children, according to a Pew Research Center survey.
3. Nearly half miss events their children are involved in.
4. Majorities say they don't have time for hobbies, socializing, exercising or relaxing.
5. Most handle family responsibilities during the workday and do work during family time.
6. Yet even though they're stretched thin, they also wouldn't necessarily choose another arrangement.
7. Their challenge, the results suggest, is having enough time and flexibility to do it all.
8. Work is set up assuming there's a parent at home, someone who's picking up the rest of your life and making those ends meet.
9. Mothers find it hardest to balance work and family.
10. Fathers also feel crunched for time, reflecting the fact that gender roles in families are less fixed today and fathers are more involved in child care.
11. Those callouts are used against you. You get written up or you get let go.
12. Parents describe being pulled in two directions at once.
13. Having the flexibility to handle tasks at different times can make the juggle feel more doable.

## 4. Vocabulary and Useful Sentences

### socializing
- Meaning: interacting with others in a friendly, informal way for companionship and enjoyment.
- Korean: 사람들과 어울려 시간을 보내는 것, 친목 활동.
- Useful sentence: Majorities say they don't have time for hobbies, socializing, exercising or relaxing.
- Sentence Korean: 대다수는 취미, 사람들과 어울리는 시간, 운동, 휴식을 위한 시간이 없다고 말한다.
- Note: 한국어의 "사교 활동"보다 자연스럽게는 "친구나 사람들과 편하게 어울려 노는 것"에 가깝다.

### be stretched thin / be spread thin
- Meaning: to have insufficient resources, such as time, energy, or money, to meet all demands.
- Korean: 무리해서 여력이 없다, 시간이나 에너지가 부족하다.
- Useful sentence: You'll burn out if you stretch yourself too thin.
- Sentence Korean: 너무 무리하면 번아웃이 올 거야.
- Note: 사람의 시간과 에너지가 여러 방향으로 나뉘어 더 이상 감당하기 어려운 상태를 말한다.

### assuming that
- Meaning: supposing that something is true; if we accept that something is true.
- Korean: ~라고 가정하면, ~라는 전제하에.
- Useful sentence: Assuming that it doesn't rain, we will host the picnic outside.
- Sentence Korean: 비가 오지 않는다는 가정하에 우리는 야외에서 피크닉을 열 거예요.
- Note: 논리나 계획의 전제를 세울 때 쓰는 조건 연결 표현이다.

### make ends meet
- Meaning: to earn just enough money to cover basic living expenses.
- Korean: 겨우 먹고살 만큼 벌다; 빠듯하게 살림을 이어가다.
- Useful sentence: Even with two jobs, she barely manages to make ends meet.
- Sentence Korean: 직업이 두 개 있어도 그녀는 겨우 생계를 유지한다.
- Note: 보통 돈에 대해 쓰지만, 기사에서는 집안이 돌아가도록 빠듯하게 살림과 생활을 떠맡는 의미로 확장되어 쓰였다.

### invest time, energy, or resources
- Meaning: to dedicate money, time, effort, or resources to something with the expectation of future benefit.
- Korean: 시간, 노력, 돈, 자원을 투자하다.
- Useful sentence: Parents invest a lot of emotional and physical energy into raising children.
- Sentence Korean: 부모들은 아이를 키우는 데 많은 정신적, 물리적 에너지를 투자한다.
- Note: 돈뿐 아니라 시간, 노력, 감정적 에너지를 쏟는다는 의미로도 자주 쓰인다.

### feel crunched for time
- Meaning: to feel strong pressure because there is not enough time.
- Korean: 시간이 부족해서 압박감을 느끼다.
- Useful sentence: Fathers also feel crunched for time.
- Sentence Korean: 아버지들도 시간이 부족하다는 압박감을 느낀다.
- Note: crunch time은 시험이나 마감처럼 시간이 얼마 남지 않은 중요한 시기를 말할 때도 쓰인다.

### call out / call out sick
- Meaning: to contact the workplace to report that you will not be coming to work, usually because you are sick.
- Korean: 결근하겠다고 알리다, 병가를 내겠다고 연락하다.
- Useful sentence: Are you going to call out? Are you going to be late?
- Sentence Korean: 결근하겠다고 할 건가요? 지각할 건가요?
- Note: 보통 계획된 휴가가 아니라 갑자기 직장에 못 간다고 알리는 상황에서 쓴다.

### be written up / get written up
- Meaning: to receive a formal, documented reprimand from an employer or authority figure.
- Korean: 공식적인 경고를 받다, 징계를 받다, 경위서를 쓰게 되다.
- Useful sentence: I need to hurry, or I'll get written up for being late again.
- Sentence Korean: 서둘러야 해. 안 그러면 또 지각했다고 경고를 받을 수도 있어.
- Note: 회사 규정 위반, 지각, 업무 수행 문제 등이 공식 기록으로 남는 상황을 말한다.

### get let go
- Meaning: to get fired from a job.
- Korean: 해고당하다.
- Useful sentence: You get written up or you get let go.
- Sentence Korean: 경고를 받거나 해고당하게 된다.
- Note: fired보다 완곡하게 들릴 수 있지만, 실제 의미는 직장에서 해고되는 것이다.

### juggle
- Meaning: to balance multiple tasks or responsibilities at the same time.
- Korean: 여러 일을 동시에 해내다, 병행하다.
- Useful sentence: Having the flexibility to handle tasks at different times can make the juggle feel more doable.
- Sentence Korean: 일을 다른 시간대에 처리할 수 있는 유연성이 있으면 여러 일을 병행하는 것이 더 할 만하게 느껴질 수 있다.
- Note: 공 여러 개를 떨어뜨리지 않고 돌리는 juggling에서 나온 비유로, 일과 육아를 동시에 감당하는 상황에 잘 어울린다.

### doable
- Meaning: feasible, manageable, achievable, or capable of being successfully completed.
- Korean: 할 만한, 해낼 수 있는.
- Useful sentence: Having the flexibility to handle tasks at different times can make the juggle feel more doable.
- Sentence Korean: 일을 다른 시간대에 처리할 수 있는 유연성이 있으면 여러 일을 병행하는 것이 더 할 만하게 느껴질 수 있다.
- Note: 어렵지만 현실적으로 해볼 수 있는 일이라는 느낌을 준다.
