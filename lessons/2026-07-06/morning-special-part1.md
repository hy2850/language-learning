## 2. Today's Reckless Sentence (오늘의 무모한 문장)

### Prompt
- Korean: 이렇게 발 벗고 나서줘서 고마워.
- Intent / nuance: 상대가 꼭 해야 하는 일이 아닌데도 먼저 나서서, 기대 이상으로 적극적으로 도와준 것에 대한 감사. 단순히 "support"보다 "go out of your way," "step up," "go the extra mile"처럼 자발성과 추가 노력을 살리는 표현이 더 잘 맞음.

### Best Listener Answers
1. Thank you for going the extra mile.
2. Thanks for going all out for me.
3. Thank you for going out of your way to help me.
4. Thank you for stepping up to help.
5. Thank you for rolling up your sleeves and helping me.
6. Thanks for jumping in and helping.
7. Thanks for bending over backwards to help me.

### Winner / Selected Answer
- Winner: 심경환 — Thank you for going out of your way to help me.
- Winner: 서미숙 — Thank you for stepping up to help.
- Why it works: 두 문장 모두 상대가 일부러 수고를 감수하거나 먼저 나서서 도와줬다는 뉘앙스를 담아 "발 벗고 나서다"의 자발성과 적극성을 잘 살림.

### Host Suggestions / Endorsed Lines
- Thank you for all of your help.
- Thank you for everything.
- Thank you for helping me despite having your hands full.
- Thank you for going above and beyond.
- Thank you for going to bat for me.
- Thanks for going out of your way to help.
- Thanks for stepping up like this.

## 3. Five Headlines

### Headline 1
- Topic: K-Food Plus exports hit a record high
- Main sentence: Exports of K-Food Plus products, which include agricultural and food items as well as farm equipment, surged to a record $7 billion in the first half of this year, growing over 4 percent from the same period last year.
- Korean summary: 올해 상반기 K-Food Plus 수출액이 70억 달러를 넘어서며 역대 최고치를 기록했다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - K-Food Plus products:
    - meaning: a broad category of food-related exports, including agricultural and food items, farm equipment, pet food, and veterinary medicines
    - Korean: 농식품과 농산업 관련 수출품을 폭넓게 묶은 항목
  - farm equipment:
    - meaning: equipment used for farming and agricultural work, such as tractors, tools, machines, and irrigation systems
    - Korean: 농기구 / 농기계
  - from the same period last year:
    - meaning: compared with the same time span in the previous year; year on year or year over year
    - Korean: 전년 동기 대비
- Useful sentences:
  - Electricity demand increased from the same period last year because of the unusually hot summer.
    - Korean: 이례적으로 더운 여름 때문에 전력 수요가 작년 같은 기간에 비해 증가했다.
- Note: 경제 지표를 설명할 때 "from the same period last year"는 전년 동기 대비 변화를 말하는 핵심 표현.

### Headline 2
- Topic: Ganjang gejang named the world's best crab dish
- Main sentence: Global food magazine Taste Atlas has ranked Korea's ganjang gejang, or soy sauce marinated crab, number one on its World's Best Crab Dishes list, rating it 4.2 out of 5.
- Korean summary: Taste Atlas의 세계 게 요리 순위에서 한국의 간장게장이 1위에 올랐다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - soy sauce marinated crab:
    - meaning: crab seasoned or preserved in soy sauce; the English description for ganjang gejang
    - Korean: 간장에 절인 게 / 간장게장
  - marinate:
    - meaning: to coat or season food before cooking or serving it to add flavor
    - Korean: 양념하다 / 절이다
  - rate:
    - meaning: to evaluate, judge, or give a score
    - Korean: 평가하다 / 점수를 주다
- Useful sentences:
  - On the app, you can rate the driver.
    - Korean: 앱에서 운전기사를 평가할 수 있다.
  - Safety is rated as the construction company's top priority.
    - Korean: 안전은 그 건설 회사의 최우선 순위로 평가된다.
- Note: 음식 소개에서는 원어명을 그대로 두고 "soy sauce marinated crab"처럼 재료와 조리 방식을 덧붙이면 자연스럽게 설명할 수 있음.

### Headline 3
- Topic: OPEC Plus countries to raise crude oil production targets
- Main sentence: Seven OPEC and OPEC Plus member countries are projected to increase crude oil production targets for a fifth consecutive month by roughly 188,000 barrels per day.
- Korean summary: OPEC 및 OPEC Plus 소속 7개국이 다섯 달 연속 원유 생산 목표치를 하루 약 18만 8천 배럴 늘릴 전망이다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - crude oil:
    - meaning: petroleum that comes out of the ground before it has been processed
    - Korean: 원유
  - roughly:
    - meaning: approximately, about, or close to a number when it is not exact
    - Korean: 대략 / 약
- Useful sentences:
  - The country's population has grown by roughly ten percent over the past decade.
    - Korean: 그 나라의 인구는 지난 10년 동안 대략 10퍼센트 늘었다.
- Note: "roughly"는 수치가 정확하지 않거나 반올림한 숫자를 제시할 때 뉴스 영어에서 자주 쓰임.

### Headline 4
- Topic: Rare U.S. Declaration of Independence copy found in the UK
- Main sentence: A rare 1776 printing of the U.S. Declaration of Independence has been discovered in the UK, tucked inside the records of a captured American ship, making it one of only eleven known surviving copies and the only one found on foreign soil.
- Korean summary: 1776년에 인쇄된 미국 독립선언서 희귀본이 영국에서 발견됐으며, 현존하는 11부 중 미국 밖에서 발견된 유일한 사본으로 알려졌다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - tucked inside:
    - meaning: placed neatly or partly hidden inside something
    - Korean: 안에 쏙 넣어져 / 숨겨져
  - tuck:
    - meaning: to put something inside or close to something so it is protected, hidden, or secure
    - Korean: 쏙 집어넣다 / 단단히 끼워 넣다
  - foreign soil:
    - meaning: land or territory outside one's own country
    - Korean: 타국의 땅 / 해외
- Useful sentences:
  - She found an old photograph tucked inside her grandmother's diary.
    - Korean: 그녀는 할머니의 일기장 속에 들어 있던 오래된 사진을 발견했다.
  - The photograph was tucked inside the diary.
    - Korean: 그 사진은 일기장 안에 들어 있었다.
- Note: "tucked inside"는 단순히 "inside"보다 어딘가에 조심스럽게 넣어 두었거나 숨겨져 있었다는 느낌을 줌.

### Headline 5
- Topic: Warm bedroom temperatures may strain the heart during sleep
- Main sentence: A study finds that bedroom temperatures above 24 degrees Celsius during the summer can place greater strain on the heart during sleep.
- Korean summary: 여름철 침실 온도가 섭씨 24도를 넘으면 수면 중 심장에 더 큰 부담을 줄 수 있다는 연구 결과가 나왔다.
- Useful vocabulary:
  - place:
    - meaning: to cause or impose something, such as a burden, pressure, or demand
    - Korean: 가하다 / 부과하다
  - strain:
    - meaning: pressure, stress, or a burden placed on a person or thing
    - Korean: 부담 / 무리 / 압박
  - place a strain on:
    - meaning: to put pressure or stress on someone or something
    - Korean: ~에 부담을 주다
- Useful sentences:
  - It can place a big strain on you.
    - Korean: 그것은 당신에게 큰 부담을 줄 수 있다.
  - Long working hours can place a heavy burden on employees.
    - Korean: 긴 근무 시간은 직원들에게 큰 부담을 줄 수 있다.
- Note: "place a strain/burden on"은 건강, 경제, 업무 환경처럼 부담이 가해지는 상황을 설명할 때 유용한 패턴.

## 4. Final Main Expressions Recap

1. from the same period last year
- meaning: compared with the same period in the previous year
- example: Electricity demand increased from the same period last year because of the unusually hot summer.
- Korean: 전년 동기 대비

2. rate
- meaning: to evaluate, judge, or give a score
- example: Safety is rated as the construction company's top priority.
- Korean: 평가하다 / 점수를 주다

3. roughly
- meaning: approximately; about
- example: The country's population has grown by roughly ten percent over the past decade.
- Korean: 대략 / 약

4. tucked inside
- meaning: placed or hidden inside something
- example: She found an old photograph tucked inside her grandmother's diary.
- Korean: 안에 쏙 들어 있는 / 숨겨져 있는

5. place a burden / place a strain
- meaning: to impose pressure, stress, or a burden on someone or something
- example: Long working hours can place a heavy burden on employees.
- Korean: 부담을 주다 / 무리를 가하다
