## 2. Today's Idiom
- Idiom: to be left holding the bag
- Korean meaning: 뒷감당을 하다, 독박을 쓰다

### Setup
식당 계산 상황. Chesu Jin은 식당 매니저, Shane은 손님 역할이다. 여섯 명이 함께 식사를 하고 계산을 나누기로 했지만 일행들이 화장실과 편의점에 간다며 사라져, 손님 혼자 계산서를 떠안게 된다.

### Dialogue
**Manager:** Good evening, sir. I hope you enjoyed your meal tonight.

**Customer:** Oh yes, everything was delicious. Thank you.

**Manager:** I'm so glad to hear that. I am terribly sorry to disturb your evening, but there is a matter of the bill to be finalised for your table.

**Customer:** Uh oh. I know. I'm just waiting for my friends to return. We're supposed to split the bill among the six of us, but a couple of them went out to the convenience store, and the others should be around somewhere.

**Manager:** Oh, your friends all left? Well, I understand, sir. But I've been looking at the clock, and it's been about twenty minutes since the bill was brought to your table, and you've been sitting alone with the bill. You see, the restaurant is preparing to close up the register.

**Customer:** I know, I know. I'm really sorry. I'm trying to call them right now, but none of them are answering their phones. Hey, have you checked the restrooms by chance? My friend Lydia specifically said she's going in there before we paid.

**Manager:** Well, actually, sir, our staff already checked the restrooms. There's no one in them.

**Customer:** Nobody's in there? What? Wow. Okay. Let me try calling them again, please. Just give me a couple minutes to figure this out.

**Manager:** Very well, sir. Take your time. I'll be back in just a few minutes.

**Customer:** Thank you. Come on! Pick up! Pick up! Why is everyone going straight to voicemail? Unbelievable! I can't believe they would actually do something like this to me. Did they seriously just run out on me? Let me check my banking app. I don't think I have enough money on this debit card right now. Let's see. Oh no! I only have twenty thousand won left. What did I even do with all my money this month? What should I do? Okay, okay. Better call my wife. Please pick up.

**Customer:** Hello, honey. Oh, thank goodness you answered. Yes, yes. You won't believe what just happened. I went out with this group to this restaurant and we ordered a huge dinner, but now they completely disappeared. Yeah, all of them. Gone. They told me they're going to the bathroom and the convenience store, but they're not answering any of my calls or texts. I've been left holding the bag, and I don't have anywhere near enough. Well, let me look. It's one hundred eighty thousand won. Yes, really! Thank you, honey. Thank you so much. Okay, thank you again. Talk to you later. Bye.

**Manager:** Hello again, sir. Is there any development with your party?

**Customer:** Yes. I will pay the bill. Just one second. Okay. I've got the cash transferred over here now. Please use this card. Thank you so much for being patient with me. I sincerely apologize for the massive inconvenience.

**Manager:** There's nothing to worry about at all, sir. We understand completely. Let me run this through for you.

### Meaning
다른 사람들이 떠나거나 책임을 회피해서, 혼자 잘못이나 비용, 책임을 떠안게 된다는 뜻이다. 이번 대화에서는 원래 함께 나눠 내야 할 식사비를 손님 혼자 처리해야 하는 상황에 쓰였다.

### Host Explanation Notes
- 여러 사람이 함께 책임져야 하는 상황에서 한 사람만 남아 뒷감당을 하게 될 때 쓴다.
- 금전적 손실, 책임, 비난을 혼자 떠안는 상황에 잘 어울린다.
- 원래는 도둑 무리가 훔친 돈가방을 들고 있다가 다른 사람들은 도망가고 한 사람만 남는 이미지에서 나온 표현이라고 설명했다.
- 범죄 상황뿐 아니라 회사 업무, 친구들과의 계산, 집 정리, 반려동물 돌보기처럼 일상적인 책임 전가 상황에도 쓸 수 있다.

### Example Usages
- My coworker promised to complete his half of the sales pitch, but called in sick, leaving me holding the bag at the boardroom meeting.
- My roommates and I hosted a massive weekend party, but they all left for early morning flights, leaving me to hold the bag and clean up the whole apartment by myself.

### Similar Expressions
- be left high and dry
- be left to clean up the mess

## 4. Vocabulary and Useful Sentences

### Burmese
- Meaning: related to Myanmar/Burma; also a common demonym for people, language, or culture from Myanmar
- Korean: 미얀마의, 버마의, 미얀마 사람의
- Useful sentence: We're talking about Burmese food.
- Sentence Korean: 우리는 미얀마 음식에 대해 이야기하고 있다.
- Note: 진행자들은 Myanmar라는 국가명을 쓰더라도 demonym으로는 Burmese가 더 흔히 쓰인다고 설명했다.

### go into something blind
- Meaning: to enter a situation without preparation, background knowledge, or enough information
- Korean: 사전 준비나 정보 없이 어떤 일을 시작하다
- Useful sentence: You should never go into a job interview blind. Research the company first.
- Sentence Korean: 면접에 사전 준비 없이 가면 절대 안 된다. 먼저 그 회사에 대해 조사해야 한다.
- Note: 고정 표현처럼 blind를 쓰며, go in cold와 비슷한 의미로 설명했다. cold turkey는 보통 습관이나 중독을 갑자기 끊는다는 뜻이라고 바로잡았다.

### well-rounded
- Meaning: balanced, complete, or having a broad range of qualities
- Korean: 균형 잡힌, 두루 갖춘, 다재다능한
- Useful sentence: The chef created a well-rounded sauce by balancing sweetness, acidity, and umami.
- Sentence Korean: 그 셰프는 단맛, 산미, 감칠맛의 균형을 맞춰 조화로운 소스를 만들었다.
- Note: 사람에게 쓰면 여러 분야를 두루 잘하는 사람이라는 뜻이고, 맛에 쓰면 여러 풍미가 조화롭다는 뜻이다.

### take center stage
- Meaning: to become the main focus or receive the most attention
- Korean: 중심이 되다, 가장 주목받다
- Useful sentence: During the election campaign, the economy took center stage.
- Sentence Korean: 선거 캠페인 기간 동안 경제 이슈가 가장 중요한 화두가 되었다.
- Note: 무대의 중앙에 서는 이미지에서 나온 표현으로, 사람뿐 아니라 주제나 이슈에도 쓸 수 있다.

### pleasantly surprised
- Meaning: surprised in a good or enjoyable way
- Korean: 기분 좋게 놀란
- Useful sentence: One couple were pleasantly surprised that well-rounded flavors, not chili heat, took center stage.
- Sentence Korean: 한 커플은 고추의 매운맛이 아니라 조화로운 풍미가 중심이 된 것에 기분 좋게 놀랐다.
- Note: 예상과 달랐지만 결과가 좋아서 기분 좋게 놀랐다는 뉘앙스다.

### eatery
- Meaning: a casual word for a restaurant
- Korean: 식당, 음식점
- Useful sentence: Opening a Burmese eatery is still very risky.
- Sentence Korean: 미얀마 식당을 여는 것은 여전히 매우 위험 부담이 크다.
- Note: restaurant보다 더 편하고 캐주얼한 느낌의 표현이라고 설명했다.

### compatriot
- Meaning: a person from the same country as you
- Korean: 동포, 같은 나라 사람
- Useful sentence: For his compatriots, two of his customers broke down in tears.
- Sentence Korean: 그의 동포들에게는 손님 두 명이 눈물을 왈칵 쏟을 만큼 감정적인 순간이었다.
- Note: 외국에서 같은 국적의 사람을 만났을 때 a compatriot이라고 할 수 있다.

### break down in tears
- Meaning: to suddenly start crying in an uncontrolled way because of strong emotion
- Korean: 울음을 터뜨리다, 눈물이 왈칵 쏟아지다
- Useful sentence: The little boy broke down in tears after losing his favorite toy.
- Sentence Korean: 가장 좋아하는 장난감을 잃어버린 뒤 그 어린 남자아이는 울음을 왈칵 터뜨렸다.
- Note: 슬픔뿐 아니라 큰 기쁨이나 감격 때문에 갑자기 눈물이 쏟아질 때도 쓸 수 있다.

### digital nomad
- Meaning: someone who works online while living or traveling in different places
- Korean: 디지털 노마드, 인터넷으로 일하며 장소에 구애받지 않는 사람
- Useful sentence: Luca, a restaurant usually packed with digital nomads, serves three of her recipes.
- Sentence Korean: 평소 디지털 노마드들로 붐비는 레스토랑 루카는 그녀의 요리법 세 가지를 선보인다.
- Note: nomad는 유목민이고, digital nomad는 인터넷 연결만 있으면 다른 나라나 장소에서도 일할 수 있는 사람을 뜻한다.

### as is often the case
- Meaning: as often happens; as is commonly true
- Korean: 흔히 그렇듯이, 자주 있는 일이지만
- Useful sentence: Many of the restaurant's service staff, who are Burmese, as is often the case in Thailand, were excited to see food from their homeland.
- Sentence Korean: 태국에서 흔히 그렇듯 미얀마 출신인 많은 레스토랑 직원들은 고국의 음식이 등장한 것을 보고 기뻐했다.
- Note: this is the case는 "이것이 사실이다", often the case는 "자주 그렇다"라는 뜻으로 설명했다.

### connotation
- Meaning: an idea, feeling, or association that a word or thing suggests beyond its literal meaning
- Korean: 함축된 의미, 뉘앙스
- Useful sentence: The advertisement uses words with strong emotional connotations to persuade consumers.
- Sentence Korean: 그 광고는 소비자를 설득하기 위해 강한 감정적 함의를 지닌 단어들을 사용한다.
- Note: slim은 긍정적인 connotation, skinny는 부정적인 connotation을 가질 수 있다는 예로 설명했다.

### inch closer to something
- Meaning: to move slowly and gradually closer to something
- Korean: 조금씩 가까워지다, 서서히 다가가다
- Useful sentence: The fighting had been inching closer to the woman's home in eastern Myanmar.
- Sentence Korean: 전투가 미얀마 동부에 있는 그 여성의 집 쪽으로 조금씩 가까워지고 있었다.
- Note: inch by inch처럼 아주 작은 단위로 조금씩 움직이는 이미지다.
