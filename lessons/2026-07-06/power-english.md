# The Hunt for Korea's Best Shaved Ice

## 1. Warm-up

Summer is bingsu season, and Alex wants to try authentic Korean shaved ice after landing in Korea. He is looking for a place that serves the real thing, not a tourist trap, and he is willing to wait in line if that means getting the best experience.

여름은 빙수의 계절이고, 한국에 도착한 알렉스는 진짜 한국식 빙수를 먹어보고 싶어 합니다. 관광객만 노리는 곳이 아니라 현지의 맛을 제대로 느낄 수 있는 곳을 찾고 있고, 최고의 경험을 위해서라면 줄 서는 것도 괜찮다고 해요.

---

## 2. Expressions

**1. the real deal**
- Something or someone that is genuine, authentic, and not fake.
- 진짜, 제대로 된 것, 믿을 만한 사람이나 것

**2. be set on (doing something)**
- To be determined to do something and unlikely to change your mind.
- ~하기로 마음을 굳히다, 꼭 ~하려고 하다

**3. beat the crowds**
- To arrive or act before many other people show up, especially to avoid waiting or congestion.
- 사람들이 몰리기 전에 먼저 가다, 붐비는 시간을 피하다

## 5. Notes

### the real deal
- If something is the real deal, it is authentic, genuine, or truly high quality. It can describe food, products, skills, or people.
- **A:** Is that family-run restaurant really famous for bingsu?
  **B:** Yes, it's the real deal. Locals line up there every summer.
- This tiny noodle shop is the real deal; locals line up before lunch.

### be set on (doing something)
- To be set on something means your mind is fixed on it. You really want it and do not plan to change course easily.
- **A:** Do you want to try another cafe?
  **B:** Not today. I'm set on going to the one you showed me.
- She's set on studying abroad next year, so she's saving every month.

### beat the crowds
- To beat the crowds means to get somewhere before it becomes busy. It is often used for restaurants, tourist spots, sales, or travel seasons.
- **A:** Why are we leaving at 7 a.m.?
  **B:** I want to beat the crowds at the beach.
- Book your tickets early if you want to beat the crowds during vacation season.

---

## 6. Power Pattern

### I don't mind
- Used to say that something is okay with you or does not bother you. It often sounds relaxed and flexible when responding to a suggestion or inconvenience.
- 괜찮아요, 저는 상관없어요, 저는 신경 안 써요

**Examples:**
1. I don't mind waiting ten more minutes if the food is that good.
2. She doesn't mind taking the bus because it's cheaper and cooler.

---

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### tourist trap
- A place that targets tourists with high prices, poor quality, or an experience that feels less authentic than advertised.
- 관광객을 상대로 비싸게 받거나 질이 떨어지는 장소, 관광객 낚시용 가게/명소

**Examples:**
1. The restaurant looked famous online, but it was a tourist trap with bland food.
2. Ask locals where they eat so you can avoid tourist traps.
