# Vinyl Record Sales Hit One Billion Dollars in the US

## 1. Warm-up

Vinyl records, CDs, and even cassette tapes are making a comeback, especially among younger buyers. Many listeners are tired of streaming costs and limited ownership, so they are turning to physical music they can collect, display, and use to support artists directly.

LP와 CD, 심지어 카세트테이프까지 젊은 구매자들을 중심으로 다시 인기를 얻고 있습니다. 스트리밍 비용과 제한적인 소유권에 지친 사람들이 직접 소장하고 전시할 수 있으며 아티스트도 바로 후원할 수 있는 실물 음반으로 눈을 돌리고 있습니다.

## 2. Expressions

**1. drive the trend**
- To lead or be the main force causing a trend to grow
- 유행을 이끌다, 흐름을 주도하다

**2. be fed up with**
- To be so annoyed or tired of something that you do not want to accept it anymore
- ~에 진절머리가 나다, ~에 질리다

**3. catch on**
- To become popular or widely accepted
- 인기를 얻다, 유행하기 시작하다

## 3. Power Article

Vinyl record sales hit one billion dollars in the US in 2025 after nineteen straight years of growth. Surprisingly, younger buyers are driving the trend; fortythree percent of CD buyers are under thirtyfive. Why the shift? People are fed up with streaming's endless playlists, rising monthly costs, and privacy worries. Physical music gives them real ownership and a way to support artists directly. Interestingly, half of vinyl buyers don't even own record players; they just collect albums as art. It's catching on beyond vinyl too. CD sales jumped seventyfour percent, and cassette sales doubled, showing people want something tangible they can hold again.

## 5. Notes

### drive the trend
- To drive the trend means to lead a trend or be the main force that makes it grow. A person, group, or idea can drive a trend.
- **A:** Why are small cameras popular again?
  **B:** Young travelers are driving the trend.
- Remote workers are driving the trend toward flexible office spaces.

### be fed up with
- To be fed up with something means to be extremely annoyed or tired of it, often after dealing with it for a long time.
- **A:** Are you keeping that phone plan?
  **B:** No, I'm fed up with all the hidden fees.
- She was fed up with waiting for the bus every morning.

### catch on
- When something catches on, it becomes popular or widely accepted. It is often used for new ideas, products, habits, or styles.
- **A:** Do people really buy reusable coffee cups there?
  **B:** Yes, the idea is catching on fast.
- The new dance caught on after a singer posted it online.

## 6. Power Pattern

### Why the shift?
- Used to ask what caused a noticeable change in a trend, opinion, behavior, or direction
- 왜 이렇게 바뀐 걸까요?, 무엇 때문에 흐름이 달라졌을까요?

**Examples:**
1. More employees are choosing four-day workweeks. Why the shift?
2. The company now focuses on smaller stores. Why the shift?

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### tangible
- Something tangible is physical and can be touched. It can also describe something real and clearly noticeable rather than abstract.
- 만질 수 있는, 실체가 있는

**Examples:**
1. A printed photo feels more tangible than an image stored on my phone.
2. The new training program produced tangible results within a month.
