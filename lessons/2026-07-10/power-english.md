# Shrinking Profit Margins: Cutting Costs to Make It Work

## 1. Warm-up

A restaurant with shrinking profit margins needs practical ways to lower its expenses without hurting the business. The owners discuss using simpler packaging, encouraging pickup orders, and shortening operating hours so they can keep the restaurant going.

수익이 줄어든 한 식당이 장사에 지장을 주지 않으면서 비용을 아낄 현실적인 방법을 찾고 있습니다. 포장을 간소화하고, 포장 주문을 유도하고, 영업시간을 줄여 식당 운영을 이어 갈 방법을 의논합니다.

## 2. Expressions

**1. cut back on**
- To use, do, or spend less of something
- ~을 줄이다, ~을 덜 쓰다

**2. opt for**
- To deliberately choose one thing from several options
- 여러 선택지 중에서 ~을 택하다

**3. make it work**
- To find a way to succeed despite a difficult or less-than-ideal situation
- 여건이 좋지 않아도 어떻게든 해내다, 잘 굴러가게 만들다

## 5. Notes

### cut back on
- To cut back on something means to reduce how much of it you use, do, or spend. It is often used when talking about expenses, food, habits, or consumption.
- **A:** Why are you making coffee at home now?
  **B:** I'm trying to cut back on eating and drinking out.
- The company cut back on business travel to lower its expenses.

### opt for
- To opt for something means to choose it deliberately from the available alternatives. It often suggests that you considered the options before deciding.
- **A:** Do you want the monthly plan or the yearly one?
  **B:** I'll opt for the monthly plan because it's more flexible.
- We opted for the earlier train so we wouldn't be late.

### make it work
- To make it work means to find a practical way to succeed even when the situation is difficult or not ideal. It often involves flexibility or creativity.
- **A:** We only have two days to finish the presentation.
  **B:** It's tight, but we'll make it work.
- They had a small budget, but they made it work by borrowing equipment.

## 6. Power Pattern

### every bit helps
- Used to say that even a very small amount or effort is useful when working toward a goal
- 조금이라도 보탬이 된다, 티끌 모아 태산이다

**Examples:**
1. Put whatever you can into your savings account—every bit helps.
2. We only collected a few cans today, but every bit helps the food bank.

---

## 7. Power Vocabulary

### pickup orders
- Orders placed in advance that customers collect themselves instead of having them delivered
- 고객이 미리 주문한 뒤 직접 찾아가는 포장 주문

**Examples:**
1. The restaurant offers a discount on pickup orders placed through its app.
2. All pickup orders will be ready at the counter near the entrance.
